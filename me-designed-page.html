03 / Projects

Engineering in practice.

Design, build, measure, and refine.

01Spring 2026

Closed-Loop Power Converter Control and PCB Design

Objective
Evaluate closed-loop converter control, output voltage regulation, and 100 Hz ripple attenuation.
My contribution
Designed the KiCad PCB around current paths, grounding, and routing constraints. Modelled Type I and Type III compensators in PLECS and implemented closed-loop control. Compared simulation and laboratory measurements, finding close overall agreement while investigating residual low-frequency noise in the Type I configuration.
Tools used
  • KiCad
  • PLECS
02Autumn 2025

Heart Rate and Blood Oxygen (SpO₂) Detection System

Objective
Acquire and process PPG signals to estimate heart rate and calculate blood oxygen saturation.
My contribution
Designed the transimpedance amplifier, signal conditioning, and filtering stages; applied FFT analysis to heart rate and signal noise. Implemented STM32 calculations using ADC and DMA, and tested CPLD decoding for a common-anode seven-segment display. Contributed to PCB layout, soldering, and debugging.
Tools used
  • STM32
  • ADC & DMA
  • FFT analysis
  • CPLD
032024–2025

Laboratory Vehicle Design

Objective
Assemble and integrate a laboratory vehicle with motor drives and remote control.
My contribution
Completed assembly and hardware connections for motors, drivers, and a Raspberry Pi control board. Implemented remote control and debugging through UART and I2C. Supported later integration of line following, image recognition, and real-time status display.
Tools used
  • Raspberry Pi
  • UART
  • I2C
